# Milestone Studio Visual Overhaul

## Reference Signals

- Awwwards luxury references repeatedly surface large photographic entrances, disciplined typography, minimal navigation, immersive gallery/portfolio layouts, and smooth transitions. Their luxury index currently includes Camper & Nicholsons and Tribe Stays among the reference set.
- Camper & Nicholsons and Blue More Yachting informed the premium editorial direction: restrained nav, strong image-led first screens, and high-trust service cues.
- Tribe Stays informed the student-market side: younger audience, accommodation/student lifestyle cues, direct conversion paths, and practical content grouping.
- Web performance guidance from web.dev shaped the gallery direction: use real images, keep initial content lightweight, and lazy-load images that are below the first viewport.
- WCAG contrast guidance shaped the flat palette: normal text should meet 4.5:1 contrast, with large text at 3:1.

## Four Redesign Directions

1. **Milestone Studio**  
   Editorial, portfolio-first, and polished. Best fit for the current implementation because the product centers student work, projects, galleries, and verified records.

2. **Milestone House**  
   Community-led and warm. Best fit if the product becomes a school-family portal with stronger parent trust and school identity.

3. **Milestone Atlas**  
   Opportunity-led and navigational. Best fit if scholarships, pathways, and recommendations become the primary differentiator.

4. **Milestone Ledger**  
   Credential-led and administrative. Best fit if verification, moderation, records, and reporting become the main selling point for institutions.

## Implemented Direction

The app now uses **Milestone Studio** as the primary brand. The logo is a flat monogram with three milestone dots, designed to work at app-icon, navbar, and favicon sizes. The visual system avoids gradients entirely, relying on flat ink, ivory, green, clay, and muted-gold color relationships.

## Gallery Direction

The gallery pattern changed from uniform cards toward a quieter editorial mosaic: the first item receives more visual weight, supporting items remain scannable, and full media opens through the existing lightbox/dialog flows. This preserves usability while giving school events and student work a more premium presentation.
